Deprecated Binary Authorization legacy continuous validation (CV) is deprecated and will no longer be available on Google Cloud after May 1, 2025. You can instead use continuous validation with check-based platform policies. To learn how to migrate to check-based platform policies, see Legacy continuous validation deprecation and shutdown.
